How it works

The UCPH phone book doesn't say when someone was hired. This site therefore keeps copies of the phone book taken on different days ("downloads") and compares them: anyone in the latest download who was not in an earlier one is new.

Monthly routine

  1. Update the data (needs access to the project on GitHub): open the update page, click Run workflow, then the green Run workflow button. After about 15 minutes this site shows the new download.
  2. New staff tab: pick the previous download to see who joined since, and download the Excel file.
  3. Compare with our contacts tab: choose a fresh export of our contacts and compare it with only new academic staff since the previous download.

Reading the comparison

  • High the same full name, and an unusual one: very likely the same person.
  • Medium the same name, but a fairly common one: probably right, check it.
  • Low a common name shared by several people, or a slightly different name (e.g. a missing middle name): check carefully.
  • Already at UCPH at signup: the contact used a UCPH email address (staff or student) in our database, so they were at UCPH before.

In the Excel file the two names are side by side, and clicking either one searches for that person on the web. The method sheet explains every rule.

Who counts as academic staff

Professors (incl. clinical and associate/assistant professors), postdocs, PhD students, researchers, research assistants and lecturers. Guests, visiting researchers, emeriti and teaching assistants are included only when you tick the box.

Privacy

The staff lists come from the public UCPH phone book; this site publishes only names, job titles, departments and links to UCPH profile pages (no phone numbers, addresses or email addresses). Contact files you compare are processed only in your browser. Treat downloaded files as personal data under the GDPR.
